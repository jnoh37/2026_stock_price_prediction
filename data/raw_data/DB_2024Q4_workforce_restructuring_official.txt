Ongoing delivery of efficiency measures

Workforce restructuring in 2024:
- Approximately 3,500 roles eliminated
- 1,800 contractors removed
- Hired 1,300 technology specialists
- Added 400 revenue-generating roles

Optimization initiatives in Germany.
Technology architecture and infrastructure efficiency.
Front-to-back setup re-design and automation of processes.

Cost management agenda for 2025:

Focus on delivering incremental savings towards the €2.5bn efficiency objective, with in-year savings in 2025 to come from:
- Germany optimization (€0.2bn)
- IT and infrastructure optimization (€0.3bn)
- Front-to-back re-design and automation of processes (€0.2bn)

Targeted cost-income ratio reset to below 65% in 2025, delivering significant operating leverage, with franchise growth achieved with flat operating expenses.
